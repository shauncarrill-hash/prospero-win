prospero-win-sender.exe

  is part of prospero-win, LGPL-2.1-or-later (LGPL-2.1.txt). Original project:
  https://github.com/mpereiraesaa/prospero-win
  This build's source, with the sender and the media DLLs' build scripts:
  https://github.com/shauncarrill-hash/prospero-win/tree/sender-gui
  (commit 15c553b87eb6705bd54741b11e2acc28e8aaf214)
